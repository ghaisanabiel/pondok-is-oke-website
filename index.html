"We built the first bungalow in 1998, on the edge of my father's rice field. The field is still there — you can hear it before you see it."

Pondok Isoke sits where the coffee plantations give way to sawah, on the quiet Licin side of Banyuwangi. Most guests come for the Ijen blue fire trek and stay for the rice paddies, the home-cooked breakfast, and the fact that nobody is trying to sell them anything.

There's no restaurant menu — you eat what the kitchen is cooking that day, usually with vegetables from the garden. Motorbikes and trekking guides can be arranged at the front desk, but nothing is pushed on you at check-in.

Getting here

Pondok Isoke is in Licin, on the Banyuwangi side of Ijen — about 45 minutes from Banyuwangi city, and the usual starting point for the midnight drive up to the crater.

  • Ijen crater trailhead (Paltuding) — approx. 45 min drive
  • Banyuwangi city / train station — approx. 45 min drive
  • Banyuwangi Airport (BWX) — approx. 1 hr drive
  • Ketapang ferry port (to Bali) — approx. 1 hr 15 min drive
